Granger Causality
Does past renewable feed-in predict future stock volatility? · Lags 1–3 trading days · α = 0.05
What is Granger Causality?
If past values of renewable feed-in X help predict current volatility Y — beyond what Y alone predicts — then X is said to Granger-cause Y.
10:00 AM · Low wind output detected
11:00 AM · Power price rises
01:00 PM · Stock volatility increases
Findings Summary
✓ SIE (Siemens) — significant at lag 1 & 2. Feed-in predicts volatility 1–2 days ahead.
✓ EOAN (E.ON) — significant at lag 2. Delayed effect: 2 trading days later.
✗ BMW, MBG, VOW3 — not significant. Automotive stocks less directly exposed to electricity prices.
✗ RWE — not significant. Possibly already priced-in through energy futures markets.
